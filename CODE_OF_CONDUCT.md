# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person means well. Keep it about the code.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What helps

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new
- Taking criticism of your code without taking it personally
- Giving criticism of someone else's code kindly and with a reason
- Accepting that the maintainer may say no to an idea, and that this is not personal either

## What does not

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Insults, personal attacks, and trolling
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Anything else you would not want quoted back to you

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to julien.schweter@proton.me. Reports stay private. I will look into every one and respond, and I will not name the reporter to the person reported.

Consequences range from a warning to a permanent ban from the project, depending on what happened.
